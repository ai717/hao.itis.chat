Binance new users get up to $100 in fee rebate vouchers

Complete new user tasks to get up to 100 USD spot trading fee rebate vouchers (rebated next day, not cash)

Crypto · Available in select countries (excl. US, UK, Canada, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, etc.) · Deadline：No deadline stated

How to claim

  1. Register Binance and complete KYC identity verification
  2. View and complete three new user tasks in App's Rewards Hub (varies by region)
  3. Click "Use" button on rebate voucher within specified days to activate

Eligibility：New registered users, complete KYC identity verification, not US/UK/Philippines/Pakistan/Bangladesh/Malaysia/Singapore/Canada/Japan users, non-API trading, non-corporate accounts, non-Lite invitation registration

Region：Available in select countries (excl. US, UK, Canada, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, etc.)

Watch out：Non-cash vouchers, no value if not trading; specific tasks and claim countdown subject to Rewards Hub display; Binance App participation only, API/corporate accounts/Lite invitation registration not eligible

Source quote

The official programme for new users is called Welcome Rewards. According to Binance's help centre (updated 1 April 2026), new users can get up to 100 USD in trading fee rebate vouchers. That's not money in your account: a voucher refunds part of your spot trading fees the following day.
