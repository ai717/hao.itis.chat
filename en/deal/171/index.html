Google Cloud New Customers $300 Free Credit

New customers get $300 free credit to try Google Cloud products and build proofs of concept, no charges before upgrading to paid

Cloud & VPS · Available worldwide · Deadline：No deadline stated

How to claim

  1. Visit https://cloud.google.com/free click Get started for free
  2. Sign in or create a Google account, fill in billing address and bind payment method to verify identity
  3. After completing registration, automatically receive $300 credit

Eligibility：New customers who have never used Google Cloud, must bind a valid payment method

Region：Available worldwide

Watch out：No charges during trial, pay-as-you-go only after manually upgrading to paid account, no auto-renewal charges; starting November 16, new sign-ups or upgrades to paid accounts after that date cannot use credits to offset Gemini platform fees—accounts registered and upgraded before November 16 can continue using remaining credits, today is the last window
